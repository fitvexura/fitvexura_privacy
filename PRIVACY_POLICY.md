# Privacy Policy for FitVexura

**Last updated:** October 30, 2026  
**Effective date:** October 30, 2026

FitVexura (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) is a fitness tracking application for Android and iOS. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

By using FitVexura you agree to this policy.

---

## 1. Information we collect

### Account data
- Email address and password (handled by Supabase Auth).
- Optional display name.

### Fitness data you enter
- Workouts (exercises, sets, reps, weight, RPE, rest times, notes).
- Personal records, templates, and training programs.
- Body metrics (weight, measurements) if you choose to log them.
- Goals, challenges, recovery scores, sleep logs, and injury notes.
- Progress photos (stored only if you add them; camera/gallery permission required).

### Device & activity data
- Step counts and activity recognition (only when you grant permission).
- Basic device information needed for notifications and crash diagnostics.

### AI Coach
- Messages you send to the in-app coach.
- These messages (plus limited training context such as recovery score and recent volume) may be sent to **Google Gemini** to generate coaching replies.
- Your Gemini API key, if configured, is stored only on your device and is never sent to our servers.

We do **not** sell your personal data.

---

## 2. How we use information

- Provide workout tracking, history, analytics, progress, and social features.
- Authenticate your account and sync data across your devices.
- Power the AI coach responses (via Google Gemini when a key is available).
- Send local or push notifications you enable (workout reminders, completion, etc.).
- Improve reliability, fix bugs, and maintain the service.

---

## 3. Storage and security

- Cloud data is stored with **Supabase** (authentication + database) using encrypted connections (HTTPS).
- Local data (preferences, offline queue, optional API key) stays on your device.
- Access to your account is protected by login. Use a strong, unique password.
- We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures; no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

---

## 4. Permissions

FitVexura may request:

| Permission              | Purpose                                      |
|-------------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| Internet                | Sync, AI coach, notifications                |
| Activity recognition    | Step counting (optional)                     |
| Notifications           | Reminders and workout alerts (optional)      |
| Camera / Photos         | Progress photos (optional)                   |
| Foreground service      | Rest timer / lock-screen countdown           |

You can deny or revoke permissions in system settings. Core logging still works without optional permissions.

## 5. Third-party services

- **Supabase** — authentication and database hosting.  
  Privacy: https://supabase.com/privacy
- **Google Gemini (Google AI)** — optional AI coaching replies.  
  Privacy: https://policies.google.com/privacy  
  Data sent: your coach messages + limited training context. Not used for advertising by us.
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging** (if enabled) — push notification delivery.
- **Google Play / Apple App Store** — may collect standard install and analytics data under their own policies.

We do not control third-party policies. Review them for more detail.

## 6. Data retention and deletion

- Account and workout data remain while your account is active.
- You may delete your account from within the app (or by contacting us). After deletion, associated cloud data is removed or anonymized within a reasonable period, subject to legal retention requirements.
- Local data is removed when you uninstall the app.

## 7. Children’s privacy

FitVexura is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.
